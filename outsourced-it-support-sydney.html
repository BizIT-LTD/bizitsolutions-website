FAQ

Outsourced IT support questions

Is outsourced IT support cheaper than hiring internally?

Outsourced support avoids the cost of a full-time salary, benefits, training and covering leave, while still providing access to a broader range of skills.

What happens if our IT person leaves — can outsourcing help?

Yes. Outsourced IT support can take over access, documentation and day-to-day operations, reducing the risk of relying on a single internal person.

Can outsourced IT scale as we grow?

Yes. Support can expand from basic help desk assistance into managed services, security and IT management as your business grows.

Do you provide a dedicated point of contact?

Yes. Outsourced support includes a clear point of contact for your team, backed by broader technical resources when needed.

Can outsourced support work alongside an existing internal person?

Yes. Outsourced support can complement an internal team member by covering specialist areas, overflow work or backup cover.
