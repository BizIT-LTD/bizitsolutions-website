FAQ

Managed service provider questions

What is a managed service provider?

A managed service provider, or MSP, takes ongoing responsibility for monitoring, maintaining and supporting your IT environment, rather than only responding when something breaks.

How is an MSP different from break-fix support?

Break-fix support responds to issues as they occur. An MSP relationship includes proactive monitoring, maintenance and administration intended to prevent issues before they disrupt your business.

What is included in an MSP agreement?

An MSP agreement can include help desk support, monitoring, patch management, Microsoft 365 administration, vendor coordination and regular reporting and reviews.

How do I switch MSPs without disruption?

BizIT Solutions can review your current environment, document access and systems, and plan a transition that minimises disruption to your team.

Can an MSP support Microsoft 365 and security?

Yes. Microsoft 365 administration and practical cyber security controls are typically core parts of an MSP relationship.
