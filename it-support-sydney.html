Everyday IT, handled

Support for the technology your team uses every day

BizIT Solutions resolves user issues, login and access problems, Microsoft 365 and email faults, device problems and vendor questions for small and growing Sydney businesses. We focus on clear communication and fixes that stop the same issue coming back.

IT support can be used on its own or as part of managed IT services, where monitoring, patching, documentation and security improvements are included.

Common support requests

  • Slow or unreliable computers
  • Outlook, email and Microsoft 365 issues
  • Login, MFA and password problems
  • New starters and leavers
  • Printers, Wi-Fi and office equipment
  • Escalations with software and internet suppliers

Three ways to get help

Helpdesk, remote support and onsite visits

Most issues are fixed fastest remotely. When they are not, they escalate to a site visit without your staff needing to start again.

IT helpdesk

A single, agreed way for staff to log requests, by email or ticket, so every issue is tracked, prioritised and followed up. Problems that stop people working come first, and recurring issues are spotted and fixed at the root.

Remote IT support

Secure, permissioned remote sessions for software, account, Microsoft 365 and device configuration issues on Windows and Mac. Remote support suits hybrid teams and businesses with more than one office, in Sydney or elsewhere.

Onsite IT support

Technician visits by appointment for hardware faults, network and Wi-Fi work, new office setups, relocations and device installation. Onsite work is scoped and quoted before anyone attends, so you know what to expect.

Where we support

Onsite across Sydney, remote wherever your team works

Onsite support covers the Sydney CBD, North Sydney, the North Shore, Northern Beaches, Hills District and surrounding areas. For businesses on the upper North Shore, see our North Shore IT support page.

Remote support and helpdesk services are coordinated from Sydney and delivered with our global technical team, so staff in other states or overseas get the same support as your Sydney office.

Remote or onsite?

  • Usually remote: email, Microsoft 365, accounts, software and settings
  • Usually onsite: hardware faults, cabling, network equipment and office moves
  • Either way: one helpdesk, one history of the issue

FAQ

IT support questions

What types of IT support do you provide?

We support user and device issues, Microsoft 365 and email, login and access problems, onboarding and offboarding, office equipment and escalations with your technology suppliers.

How do staff log a helpdesk request?

Staff log requests through one agreed process, such as email or a ticketing tool, so every issue is tracked, prioritised and followed up.

What can be fixed remotely and what needs a site visit?

Software, account, Microsoft 365 and most device configuration issues can usually be resolved remotely. Hardware faults, cabling and network equipment typically need an onsite visit.

Is remote support secure?

Yes. Remote sessions use secure, permissioned tools, and access is only used for the specific issue being resolved.

Which areas of Sydney do you cover onsite?

Onsite visits are available by appointment across the Sydney CBD, North Sydney, the North Shore, Northern Beaches, Hills District and surrounding areas.

Do you support Mac and Windows devices?

Yes. Support covers Windows and Mac devices, along with common business applications and cloud services.
