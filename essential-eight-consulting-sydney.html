FAQ

Essential Eight questions

What is Essential Eight consulting?

It helps assess your current controls, identify gaps and plan practical improvements aligned to the Australian Cyber Security Centre model.

Do small businesses need Essential Eight?

Many small businesses use the Essential Eight as a clear security framework, especially when customers, insurers or partners expect stronger controls.

Can you help implement the recommendations?

Yes. We can help implement practical controls across Microsoft 365, endpoints, access, backups, patching and documentation.

More questions

Planning this service

What does an Essential Eight gap review cover?

A review can examine MFA, patching, application control basics, administrative access, backups and other relevant control evidence.

Will we receive a practical roadmap?

Yes. Findings can be prioritised into achievable actions based on risk, dependencies and the needs of your business.
