What it is

The Essential Eight, in plain language

The Essential Eight is a set of eight mitigation strategies published by the Australian Cyber Security Centre (ACSC) to make it much harder for attackers to compromise business systems. It is measured against a maturity model, from Maturity Level Zero up to Maturity Level Three.

Government agencies, larger clients, cyber insurers and procurement teams increasingly ask small and mid-sized businesses where they sit on that model. We help you answer the question honestly and improve your position in a sensible order, without turning it into a paperwork exercise.

Read: Essential Eight for small businessRead: Microsoft 365 security checklist

The eight strategies

  • Patch applications
  • Patch operating systems
  • Multi-factor authentication
  • Restrict administrative privileges
  • Application control
  • Restrict Microsoft Office macros
  • User application hardening
  • Regular backups

FAQ

Essential Eight consulting questions

What is the Essential Eight?

The Essential Eight is a set of eight cyber security mitigation strategies published by the Australian Cyber Security Centre, measured against a maturity model from Maturity Level Zero to Maturity Level Three.

Is the Essential Eight mandatory for my business?

It is mandatory for many Australian Government entities. For private businesses it is usually not a legal requirement, but clients, tenders and cyber insurers increasingly ask about it.

Which maturity level should a small business aim for?

Maturity Level One is a practical first target for most small businesses. The right level depends on your risk, clients and contractual requirements, which we work through during scoping.

Do you work with businesses outside Sydney?

Yes. Assessments are delivered remotely for businesses across Australia, using evidence from your Microsoft 365, device management and backup systems. Onsite workshops can be arranged in Sydney.

Can you implement the fixes as well as assess?

Yes. You can implement the roadmap yourself, with your current IT provider, or with BizIT Solutions, followed by a reassessment to confirm progress.

How long does an Essential Eight assessment take?

It depends on the number of users, devices and systems in scope. We agree the scope and timeframe before work begins.
