FAQ

Microsoft 365 migration questions

How long does a Microsoft 365 migration take?

Timing depends on mailbox size, data volume and business complexity. We can provide a realistic timeline once your environment has been reviewed.

Will we lose emails or data during migration?

Migrations are planned and tested to protect against data loss, with mailbox and file data verified after the move.

Can you migrate from Google Workspace or another provider?

Yes. We can plan and support migrations from Google Workspace, other hosted email or legacy on-premises systems.

Do you handle licensing during migration?

Yes. We can review and configure the right Microsoft 365 licensing for your business as part of the migration.

What support is available after migration?

Post-migration support is available to resolve any issues and help your team settle into the new environment.
