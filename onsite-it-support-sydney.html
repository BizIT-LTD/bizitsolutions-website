FAQ

Onsite IT support questions

When is onsite support needed vs remote?

Onsite support is best for hardware faults, physical network work, office setups and issues that cannot be diagnosed or resolved remotely.

How do I book an onsite visit?

Onsite visits can be arranged by appointment. Contact BizIT Solutions to describe the issue and arrange a suitable time.

What areas of Sydney do you cover?

Onsite support covers Sydney CBD, the North Shore, Northern Beaches, Hills District and surrounding areas.

Can you help with office moves or new setups?

Yes. Onsite support can cover new office setups, relocations, cabling and device or peripheral installation.

Is there a minimum onsite call-out?

Onsite visits are scoped and quoted based on the work required, so you know what to expect before a technician attends.
