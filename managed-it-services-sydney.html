What managed IT means

One provider responsible for your IT, not just the next fault

With managed IT services, a managed service provider (MSP) takes ongoing responsibility for monitoring, maintaining and supporting your technology environment. Instead of calling someone only when something breaks, you get proactive administration, recurring checks, documentation and a plan for what should improve next.

BizIT Solutions works with Sydney businesses that have outgrown ad-hoc support, rely on several technology vendors, or depend on one person who “knows the IT”. We document your environment, take over day-to-day requests and keep Microsoft 365, devices, backups and security controls in order.

Choosing a support model

Managed IT vs break-fix vs hiring internally

Most small businesses weigh the same three options. Here is how they compare in practice.

Break-fix support

You pay when something goes wrong. It can suit very small teams with simple systems, but nobody owns the environment between incidents, documentation is rarely kept, and the same problems tend to return.

An internal IT hire

A dedicated person on site, but with salary, leave, training and a single point of failure. One generalist rarely covers Microsoft 365, security, networking and planning equally well.

Managed IT services

Clear ongoing ownership with a broader skill set behind it. Support, monitoring, security and planning are handled together, and the plan scales as you add people, sites or systems.

FAQ

Managed IT services questions

What is a managed service provider?

A managed service provider (MSP) takes ongoing responsibility for monitoring, maintaining and supporting your IT environment, rather than only responding when something breaks.

What is included in managed IT services?

Helpdesk support, monitoring, patch management, Microsoft 365 administration, endpoint security, backup checks, vendor coordination, documentation and regular reviews can all be included, scoped to your business.

Is managed IT cheaper than hiring an internal IT person?

For most small businesses, yes. Managed IT avoids the cost of a full-time salary, leave cover and training, and gives you access to a broader range of skills than one generalist can provide.

How do I switch MSPs without disruption?

We review your current environment, document admin access and suppliers, and plan the handover so your team keeps working. Urgent risks are fixed first, then improvements follow a roadmap.

Do you provide onsite support in Sydney?

Yes. Most requests are resolved remotely, and onsite visits are available by appointment across the Sydney CBD, North Shore, Northern Beaches, Hills District and surrounding areas.

Can you support staff outside Sydney?

Yes. Remote support, monitoring and Microsoft 365 administration work for staff in other states and overseas, while your account is managed from Sydney.
