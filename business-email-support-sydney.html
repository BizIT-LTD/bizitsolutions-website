FAQ

Business email support questions

Why are my emails going to spam?

Emails can be marked as spam due to sending reputation, missing authentication records or content triggers. We can review your configuration and identify the cause.

Can you fix mailbox access or permission issues?

Yes. We can troubleshoot mailbox permissions, shared access and login issues affecting Outlook and Exchange Online.

What is SPF, DKIM and DMARC and do I need them?

These are email authentication records that help prevent spoofing and improve deliverability. Most businesses benefit from having them configured correctly.

Can you set up shared mailboxes and distribution lists?

Yes. We can create and configure shared mailboxes, distribution lists and permissions to match how your team works.

How do you improve email security against phishing?

We can review spam filtering, authentication records, MFA and user permissions to reduce the risk of phishing and account compromise.
