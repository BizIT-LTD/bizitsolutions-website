FAQ

IT helpdesk questions

How do staff log a helpdesk request?

Staff can log requests through a simple, agreed process such as email or a ticketing tool, so every issue is tracked and followed up.

What is your helpdesk response time?

Requests are acknowledged promptly, with priority given to issues that are stopping staff from working.

Can the helpdesk handle Microsoft 365 issues?

Yes. The helpdesk supports Microsoft 365, email, Teams and other everyday collaboration tools.

Is helpdesk support included in managed IT plans?

Yes. Helpdesk support is typically a core part of managed IT services, alongside monitoring and administration.

Can helpdesk escalate to onsite support when needed?

Yes. Issues that cannot be resolved remotely can be escalated to an onsite visit by appointment.
