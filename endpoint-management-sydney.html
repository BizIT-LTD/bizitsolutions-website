FAQ

Endpoint management questions

What is endpoint management?

Endpoint management is the ongoing configuration, security and monitoring of laptops, desktops and mobile devices used across your business.

Can you manage both company and personal devices?

Yes. Endpoint management can cover company-owned devices and, where appropriate, personal devices accessing business data.

How do you handle onboarding and offboarding?

We can set up devices and access for new starters and revoke access and secure devices when staff leave, following a consistent process.

Can you patch and update software remotely?

Yes. Patch management can be applied remotely across managed devices to keep software current.

Does endpoint management improve security?

Yes. Consistent configuration, patching and endpoint security reduce the risk of a compromised device affecting the wider business.
