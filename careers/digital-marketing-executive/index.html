The Opportunity

We are looking for a results-driven Digital Marketing Executive to strengthen our digital presence, generate qualified B2B leads, and support business expansion into international markets. This is a hands-on role for someone who combines digital marketing expertise with strong business communication and lead-generation skills.

About BizIT Solutions

BizIT Solutions helps businesses strengthen their IT infrastructure, cybersecurity, and digital operations through technology consulting and managed IT services. Our Global Delivery Center (GDC) model supports clients across multiple geographies.

Key Responsibilities

  • B2B Lead Generation: Identify target companies, research decision-makers, and generate qualified business leads through LinkedIn, email campaigns, and other relevant channels.
  • LinkedIn Marketing: Manage professional content, outreach activities, and engagement to build brand visibility and business opportunities.
  • SEO & Website Optimization: Conduct keyword research, optimize website content, and improve organic visibility.
  • Content & Campaigns: Create compelling content for LinkedIn, websites, blogs, email campaigns, and digital marketing initiatives.
  • Lead Tracking & Analytics: Monitor campaign performance, manage prospect databases, follow up with leads, and improve conversion outcomes.

Required Skills

  • 2–3 years of hands-on digital marketing experience, preferably in B2B, IT services, technology, or SaaS.
  • Strong expertise in B2B lead generation and LinkedIn prospecting.
  • Good understanding of SEO, email marketing, and digital campaign performance.
  • Excellent written and spoken English, with strong content creation and copywriting skills.
  • Ability to research target markets, engage business decision-makers, and convert prospects into qualified opportunities.
  • Good to Have: Canva, WordPress, HubSpot, Mailchimp, SEMrush/Ahrefs, Google Tag Manager, and LinkedIn Campaign Manager.
