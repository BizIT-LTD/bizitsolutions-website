Build client relationships and develop new business for BizIT's technology services.

The opportunity

BizIT Solutions is seeking an independent business development partner to develop new business for our IT services. You will identify potential clients, build relationships, understand their business needs and generate qualified sales opportunities. You will work alongside BizIT to progress those opportunities through the sales process and support the handover into onboarding.

BizIT is responsible for technical assessments, solution design, pricing, contracts, implementation, service delivery, invoicing and account administration.

This is a commission-only contractor opportunity with no fixed salary. It suits an experienced sales professional, consultant, marketer or business networker who can develop new client relationships. You will manage your own workload and schedule, with commission payable on eligible customer business and payments received by BizIT under the agreed terms.

About BizIT Solutions

BizIT Solutions supports small and medium businesses with managed IT support, Microsoft cloud solutions, cybersecurity, infrastructure management, monitoring, backup and recovery, migrations, automation and project delivery. We focus on practical solutions that are secure, reliable and suited to each client's needs.

Key responsibilities

  • Identify and engage prospective clients within BizIT's target market and agreed territory.
  • Generate and qualify new business opportunities through networking, referrals, market research, and direct outreach.
  • Conduct discovery meetings to understand client needs, business objectives, budgets, and key decision-makers.
  • Actively promote and sell BizIT's services, lead commercial discussions and progress qualified opportunities towards new business, with BizIT approving all pricing and contractual commitments.
  • Present BizIT's services and capabilities and coordinate with the technical team for solution design and specialist advice.
  • Build and maintain strong relationships with prospects, clients, and business partners.
  • Prepare and support proposals, commercial discussions, and solution presentations in collaboration with BizIT.
  • Coordinate the handover of successful opportunities to BizIT for contracting, onboarding and delivery.
  • Maintain accurate pipeline, opportunity, and client records in accordance with BizIT's agreed processes.
  • Identify new markets, partnerships, and growth opportunities that support BizIT's business objectives.
  • Support reasonable customer follow-up during onboarding, while BizIT manages service delivery, invoicing and account administration.
  • Protect confidential information and comply with all applicable legal, privacy, and ethical requirements.

Required skills and experience

  • Proven experience finding and developing new business opportunities.
  • Confidence speaking with business owners, senior leaders, IT decision-makers, and other key stakeholders.
  • The ability to understand a client's needs and identify where BizIT's services may be a good fit.
  • Strong communication, relationship-building, presentation and follow-up skills.
  • An existing business network, or a clear plan for building one within the agreed territory.
  • Well organised, self-motivated and comfortable managing your own pipeline and activities.
  • Good judgement, professionalism, and the ability to represent BizIT positively in the market.
  • A commitment to confidentiality, privacy, and ethical business practices.

Desirable skills and experience

  • Experience selling, introducing, or referring managed IT services, cloud solutions, cybersecurity services, infrastructure, software, consulting, or professional services.
  • A general understanding of business technology and the ability to discuss client needs at a high level.
  • Experience working with small and medium-sized businesses.
  • Experience in industries such as professional services, healthcare, finance, property, retail, or education.
  • Previous experience as an independent business development partner, sales representative, consultant, introducer, or referral partner.

Commercial arrangement

This is a commission-only independent contractor opportunity with no fixed salary. Commission is payable on eligible business generated for BizIT, generally after the relevant customer payments have been received. Eligible opportunities may include recurring service revenue and one-off projects, subject to the Independent Business Development Partner Agreement.

Commission rates, eligible revenue, the duration of any recurring commission, lead attribution, payment timing and the treatment of cancellations and termination will be discussed with shortlisted candidates and agreed in writing before engagement.

Important engagement terms

The initial term is six months and may be extended by mutual agreement. Either party may terminate with 30 days' written notice, subject to the signed agreement. You will manage your own schedule, equipment and business expenses. Tax, insurance and superannuation responsibilities will be addressed in the agreement, subject to applicable law. You are not authorised to make commitments, enter into agreements or otherwise bind BizIT without prior written approval. Enquiries generated through BizIT's existing marketing, referral or business channels remain outside your scope unless otherwise agreed in writing.

Applicants should hold, or be willing to obtain, an Australian Business Number (ABN).
