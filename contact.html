What happens after you send an IT enquiry

Reaching out is the first step toward a clearer, more reliable technology setup. BizIT Solutions keeps the process straightforward — there is no obligation and no pressure, just a focused conversation about your current situation and what the most practical next IT step looks like for your business.

Most IT challenges at small-business level are more manageable than they first appear. We aim to give honest, grounded advice — whether that means a quick fix, a clear project scope or simply pointing you in the right direction.

  • We review your enquiry and confirm we understand the situation
  • We respond within one business day with initial thoughts or questions
  • A brief conversation helps define the right approach and realistic scope
  • We recommend a practical IT step suited to your business size and budget
  • No lock-in commitments on initial advisory work — we start where it makes sense
