What a virtual IT manager does

Someone accountable for IT decisions, a few days a month

A virtual IT manager, sometimes called a fractional or part-time IT manager, gives your business the planning and oversight an internal IT manager would provide, at the level you actually need. They sit between your leadership team and your technology suppliers, so decisions get made, projects get finished and risks are visible.

It suits businesses that have outgrown ad-hoc technology choices but cannot justify a full-time IT manager. It also works alongside an existing IT support provider or internal technician, adding leadership rather than replacing support.

Signs you need one

When a virtual IT manager makes sense

Nobody owns IT decisions

Renewals, purchases and security questions land with an owner or office manager who has other priorities.

Projects keep stalling

Migrations, new systems or office moves drag on because no one is coordinating suppliers and deadlines.

Suppliers aren’t held to account

Several vendors, unclear responsibilities and contracts nobody has reviewed in years.

Your IT person is leaving

Knowledge, passwords and supplier relationships need to be documented and handed over properly.

Growth or new sites

New staff, offices or states mean systems and security need planning, not just more devices.

Clients are asking about security

Tenders, insurers or larger customers want answers about MFA, backups and Essential Eight maturity.

FAQ

Virtual IT manager questions

What does a virtual IT manager do?

A virtual IT manager provides part-time IT leadership: planning the roadmap and budget, managing suppliers and projects, reviewing risks and reporting to the business.

Is a virtual IT manager the same as a fractional CIO?

They overlap. A virtual IT manager is usually more hands-on with suppliers, projects and day-to-day decisions, which suits small and mid-sized businesses better than a purely strategic role.

Can a virtual IT manager work with our existing IT provider?

Yes. Your virtual IT manager can coordinate and hold your current provider to account, or bring in BizIT Solutions managed IT services if that suits you better.

Do you work with businesses outside Sydney?

Yes. Virtual IT management is delivered remotely for businesses across Australia, with in-person sessions available in Sydney.

How many hours a month do we need?

It depends on your size, projects and how many suppliers are involved. We agree a starting level during the consultation and adjust it as your needs change.

What reporting do business leaders receive?

Reporting summarises current issues, risks, projects, supplier actions, roadmap priorities and decisions that need leadership input.
